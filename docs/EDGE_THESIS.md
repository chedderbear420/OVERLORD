# Edge Thesis

Overlord's edge is not predicting winners. Its edge is identifying and proving short-horizon mispricing caused by reaction lag, behavioral overreaction, and market microstructure inefficiency, then validating whether the opportunity survives fees, spread, liquidity, slippage, uncertainty, and risk limits.

## Mispricing Sources
- Reaction lag after sports events, lineup changes, injuries, weather, officiating events, or market-wide repricing.
- Behavioral overreaction to salient but noisy updates.
- Binary order book microstructure inefficiency, including thin depth, stale quotes, uneven YES/NO books, and wide spreads.

## Proof Requirement
A signal is valid only if all of the following pass:
- Model probability.
- Market price.
- Fee-aware expected value.
- Spread and slippage.
- Liquidity and depth.
- Timing and staleness checks.
- Replay evidence.
- Calibration history.
- Risk checks.

## Anti-Thesis
Overlord is not a tipster, winner predictor, order bot, or narrative authority. It is an evidence system for deciding whether a short-horizon price is wrong enough to matter after all costs and controls.
