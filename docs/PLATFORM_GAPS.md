# Platform Gaps

This document tracks gaps that must be closed before Overlord can move beyond research and paper workflows.

## Current Gaps
- No approved Kalshi connectivity.
- No immutable event store implementation.
- No binary order book normalizer.
- No MarketState schema.
- No EdgeSignal schema.
- No replay engine.
- No execution simulator.
- No paper ledger.
- No calibration reports.
- No risk governor implementation.
- No permission gate.
- No dashboard.
- No operator workflow.

## Promotion Gaps
Before any live mode can be proposed, Overlord needs clean data, deterministic replay, realistic execution simulation, calibrated probabilities, audited decisions, hard risk caps, manual permissions, and a kill switch.
