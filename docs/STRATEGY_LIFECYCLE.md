# Strategy Lifecycle

Strategies move through explicit research gates.

## Required Strategy Definition
Every strategy must define:
- Entry conditions.
- Exit conditions.
- Stop conditions.
- No-trade conditions.
- Risk limits.
- Required features.
- Data freshness requirements.
- Calibration requirements.

## Lifecycle
- Draft: idea and required evidence.
- Registered: schema complete, no production claims.
- Replay-tested: deterministic historical tests complete.
- Paper-tested: simulated ledger and scorecards exist.
- Calibrated: probability and edge quality reviewed.
- Governed: risk checks and permission gates defined.
- Promoted: only after explicit approval for the next phase.

## Current Limit
No strategy may be promoted to live execution in the current phase.
