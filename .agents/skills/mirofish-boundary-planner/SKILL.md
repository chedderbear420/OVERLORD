# MiroFish Boundary Planner

Plan the future MiroFish boundary while preventing narrative simulation from becoming trading authority.

Use for MiroFish adapter docs, narrative context boundaries, scenario hypothesis workflows, and integration limits.

## When Not To Use
Do not integrate MiroFish yet or treat it as source truth, price truth, model truth, or trading authority.

## Required Inputs
- Intended narrative use case.
- Required safety boundary.
- Relevant architecture docs.

2. Limit outputs to narrative hypotheses or context.
3. Require validation by data, replay, calibration, and risk.
4. Document forbidden authority.

## Outputs

## Safety Checks

- No truth-engine claims.
